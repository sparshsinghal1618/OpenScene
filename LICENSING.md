# Commercial Licensing

OpenScene is free software, released under the **GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0** (`LICENSE`). For most people that is all you will ever need: you
can use it, modify it, and share it at no cost.

A **commercial licence** is available for organisations that cannot accept the
AGPL's obligations.

## Why the AGPL might not suit you

The AGPL is a strong copyleft licence. In plain terms, if you:

- distribute a modified version of OpenScene, or
- **run a modified version so that users interact with it over a network**,

then the AGPL requires you to release the complete source code of your modified
version, under the AGPL, to those users.

That network clause (section 13) is what distinguishes the AGPL from the
ordinary GPL, and it is what makes the AGPL unsuitable for some products.

## When you need a commercial licence

You should obtain a commercial licence if you want to do any of the following
without the AGPL's source-disclosure obligations:

1. **Embed OpenScene in a closed-source product.** Ship it as part of a product
   whose source you do not publish.
2. **Offer a modified OpenScene as a hosted service** without publishing your
   modifications.
3. **Rebrand and redistribute** a modified build without releasing the source.
4. **Receive a warranty or indemnity.** The AGPL provides the software "as is"
   with no warranty. A commercial licence can include negotiated terms.

## What a commercial licence includes

Terms are negotiated per engagement, but a typical agreement covers:

- A non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual right to use, modify, and distribute
  OpenScene in closed-source form.
- An exemption from the AGPL's source-disclosure requirements (sections 5, 6
  and 13).
- Optional support, response-time commitments, and indemnification.
- Optional custom development: additional formats, effects, branding, or
  workflow integration.

Pricing depends on scope — the size of your deployment, whether you need
support, and whether custom work is involved.

## How to obtain one

Open an issue titled "Commercial licence" on the repository, or contact the
copyright holder directly. Please include:

- Your organisation and what you intend to build.
- Whether you need distribution rights, hosted-service rights, or both.
- Whether you need support, warranty, or custom development.

## Contributor contributions

OpenScene accepts outside contributions only under the agreement in `CLA.md`,
which grants the copyright holder the right to relicense contributed code. This
is what makes a commercial licence possible: without it, a single contributed
patch under the AGPL alone could not be relicensed, and the offer above would
not be enforceable.

## Frequently asked

**Do I need a commercial licence to use OpenScene internally?**
No. The AGPL permits unrestricted private use. You only need a commercial
licence if you distribute a modified version, or run one as a network service.

**Do I need one to use OpenScene commercially, unmodified?**
No. Using the software, unmodified, to edit video — including for paid work —
is permitted by the AGPL at no cost.

**What if I just want to include the unmodified installer in a product?**
Section 6 of the AGPL has conditions for conveying non-source forms. If you are
unsure, ask before shipping.

**Is this legal advice?**
No. It is a summary written for clarity. Consult your own counsel before relying
on it.
